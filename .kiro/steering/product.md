# Product

Frankenstein's Lovecraftian Village is a Unity 6 HDRP cosmic-horror village environment built for the Kiroween hackathon (Frankenstein category). The idea: stitch unlike parts into one living creature: a commercial modular asset pack, procedural layout, a sanity-driven horror director, and optional AI narration.

- Goal: a playable, atmospheric village slice plus a transparent record of how Kiro built it.
- Audience: hackathon judges and Unity developers who want a testable, modular horror-environment template.
- Tone: gothic, dread over gore. No explicit violence in content or text.
- Legal: the 'Lovecraftian Village 1.3 HDRP' pack is commercial and owned by the author. It is never committed to this repo.

- Audio rule: the web soundtrack is original and procedural (WebAudio), silent until the user opts in, with no audio files and no copyrighted music.
- Round 12 facts: scene mode and pack textures are local only; original Blender meshes are committed; no live URL, Unity run or MCP use is claimed.

See `project.md` for the mission and horror design philosophy and `agent.md` for the narrative consistency rules.
- Round 16 facts: the demo video is side by side (live web left, the author's own Unity session right, seed 1818). Own footage only, no DLNK trailer, no copyrighted music, credit DLNK. Unity cannot be installed in the sandbox (Personal licence activation is tied to the author's account): Unity code is Partially Verified (compile check) and Unverified at runtime.
- Round 19 facts: a person is 1.7 m (eye 1.6 m, door 2.0 m, scale 1), days are free exploration with seeded villagers, the vigil is kept at dusk and night, and the harbour quarter is original. The URP/HDRP packs were inspiration only. "Nivalis nights" is genre inspiration only (the author can correct this).
