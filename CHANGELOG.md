### Round 23 (part 10): grove investigation and final docs (FEAT-006)
- Investigated the FEAT-009 note (a `?pack=1` still looked right but its trees seemed to hover, and a no-pack still with the same hash showed a bright sky and no trees). Corruption comes from `corruptionLevel(dread, night)` (dread 0.75, night 0.25), and `sanity=0&night=10` gives level 6 (25 sites) while `sanity=100&night=1` gives level 0 (none). Pass `tick=1250` for night. (b) Pack models are grounded: `fitModelInto` puts the model floor on the floor of the original mesh (min y 0) for all seven kinds, and every corruption site stands on Core `groundHeight` (checked for 10 seeds, 0 mismatches). The hover look comes from the pack tree itself: its canopy is a wide jellyfish shape and only 0.5% of its vertices (root tips) reach the floor. No render fix needed; a node test now pins both facts. (c) The no-pack still was aimed away from the grove (camera at z 128 looking toward +z), and the trees in it were the ordinary grove trees, not corruption sites. From `cam3d=-13,134,0,3` the originals appear at the Core sites.
