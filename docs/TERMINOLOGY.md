# Terminology

One term per concept, in the UI (English and French), the data files and these docs. The table lives in `web/test/terminology-table.js` and `web/test/terminology.test.js` fails when a synonym appears (round 37).

| Concept | English | French (UI, no accents) | Not used |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trust | trust | confiance | mistrust |
| Suspicion | suspicion | mefiance | wariness, soupcon |
| Fear | fear | peur | fright, terror, crainte |
| Faith | faith | foi | devotion, piety |
| Loyalty | loyalty | loyaute | allegiance, fidelite |
| Obsession | obsession | obsession | fixation |
| Corruption | corruption | corruption | tainted, souille |
| Knowledge tier | knowledge tier (Nothing, Village Folklore, Cult Secrets, Forbidden Texts, Eldritch Science, Outer Truth) | niveau | knowledge level, rank |
| Outcome of a missing person | outcome (dead, escaped, recruited, transformed, hidden, displaced) | issue | fate |
| Creature tier | tier 1 to 4 | niveau | creature rank, class |

Dread (effroi in French) is a different concept from fear (peur): dread is the village-wide meter, fear is one villager's axis.
